Questions  ·  Straight answers

The questions people actually ask.

Not the ones that make us look good — the ones that come up on the phone. If yours isn't here, call and ask. We answer our own phones.

What areas do you serve?

Los Angeles County and Orange County. That includes Pasadena, Arcadia, San Gabriel, Temple City, Alhambra, Monterey Park, Cerritos, Irvine, Anaheim, and Orange, among others. If you're near the edge of that area, call and ask — the answer is often yes.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. California contractor license #1137668, Class B General Building, and we carry insurance. You can verify that yourself at cslb.ca.gov by searching the license number. You should do that with any contractor you're considering — not just us.

Will you take on a small project, like a single bathroom?

Yes. A single bathroom is a real job to us, and so is one kitchen or one closed‑off wall. Dotty started with a single kitchen and grew by word of mouth from there. Plenty of our clients started small and called us back years later about something bigger.

How much will my remodel cost?

We don't publish prices, and we'd rather tell you why than pretend it's a policy. A number without a scope attached is misleading. The same bathroom can differ several times over depending on whether the drain moves, what's behind the walls, and what you pick to put in it — so a range on a website would either be so wide it's useless or so narrow it's wrong.

What we do instead: come out for free, measure, and give you a written estimate line by line, so you can see what each decision costs before you commit to it.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes — free, and with no obligation. We come out, look at the space, and put real numbers on paper. You're not committed to anything by asking.

How quickly will you get back to me?

Usually the same day. We answer our own phones, so you're talking to someone who can actually answer questions about your project rather than a call center taking a message.

Do I need an architect before I call you?

Usually not. We design the floor plan in‑house, so the person deciding where the island or the wall goes is on the same team as the people building it. That matters most when something unexpected turns up behind a wall — it becomes a decision instead of a change order and a three‑week stall.

Some large or complex projects do still need a licensed architect or a structural engineer. We'll tell you if yours is one of them.

Does my project need a permit?

In California, work that moves a wall, a drain, or a gas line generally requires a permit, and inspections come with it. Cosmetic work — paint, flooring, swapping a vanity in place — generally doesn't. The exact rules depend on your city. We'll walk you through which parts of your project need one when we come out to measure.

Will I be dealing with the same people the whole time?

Yes. The people you meet at the consultation are on site every day until the walk‑through. That's deliberate, and it's the main reason we take on a limited number of projects at a time rather than as many as we could staff.

What kinds of projects do you do?

Kitchens, bathrooms, room and second‑story additions, ADUs and garage conversions, and whole‑home remodels and gut rebuilds. Whole‑home is the work we do most.
