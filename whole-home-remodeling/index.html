Whole‑home  ·  Los Angeles & Orange County

When the house needs rethinking, not just repairing.

This is the work we do most. A whole‑home remodel is the one project where you can fix the things that are actually wrong with a house — the hallway that eats floor space, the bedroom nobody uses because you have to walk through it, the kitchen stuck in the darkest corner. Everything is open at once, which makes it the cheapest time to move anything.

What the work involves

The plan is worth more than any finish you'll pick.

Most houses in Southern California were laid out for how people lived decades ago — formal rooms nobody sits in, a kitchen closed off from everything, a hallway spending square footage on walking. Once the walls are open, none of that has to stay. That's the real opportunity, and it's the reason we start with the floor plan rather than a finish schedule.

We draw it in‑house. That matters more here than on any other kind of project, because on a whole‑home job the plan and the build have to stay in conversation for months. When the person who drew it is on the same team as the person building it, a surprise behind a wall becomes a decision instead of a change order and a three‑week stall.

The systems are the unglamorous half. A house being remodeled top to bottom is usually also a house with original wiring, galvanized supply lines, no insulation worth the name, and a panel that can't carry what a modern kitchen asks of it. Doing that work while the walls are open costs a fraction of what it costs later.

It's also disruptive in a way a single‑room project isn't. Most people move out for at least part of a whole‑home remodel. We'd rather be straight with you about that up front than discover it together in month two.

  • Floor plan reconfiguration, drawn in‑house
  • Wall removal, beams, and structural framing
  • Kitchens and bathrooms throughout
  • Electrical rewiring and panel upgrades
  • Plumbing replacement and repipes
  • HVAC, insulation, windows, and doors
  • Flooring, millwork, and finish carpentry
  • Exterior, roofing, and hardscape as needed

How it goes

Four steps, and you meet the same people at each one.

  1. We come look

    Free, no obligation. We walk the whole house and talk about how you actually want to live in it.

  2. We draw it

    A reworked floor plan in‑house, with the trade‑offs explained. This is where the value is.

  3. Real numbers

    A written estimate against that plan, line by line, so the scope and the budget are the same conversation.

  4. We build it

    The same crew from demolition through the walk‑through, months later. We answer our own phones the whole way.
