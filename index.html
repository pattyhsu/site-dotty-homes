About

Dotty Homes builds and remodels homes across Los Angeles and Orange County — for our neighbors, mostly.

We started Dotty the way most good shops start — one kitchen. The client told a neighbor, the neighbor told another, and somewhere along the way we became a construction company. Four years later that’s still how the work comes in, and the same people you meet at the consultation are on site every day until the walk‑through.

No project is too small. We do whole‑home rebuilds and additions, and we also do the single bathroom, the closed‑off kitchen, the garage nobody has touched since 1978. If you’ve been putting something off because it felt too minor to call a contractor about — call us about it.

We like good neighbors, honest budgets, and the kind of detail nobody photographs but everyone notices. We answer our own phones, show up when we say we will, and design every floorplan ourselves.
